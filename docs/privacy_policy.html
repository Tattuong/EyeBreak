Privacy Policy – ShiftLens

ShiftLens is a private, on-device calendar for shift work. Cycles, one-day changes, notes, reminders, coins, and shop unlocks stay on this phone. We do not create an account and we do not upload a rota.

Data stored on the device

  • Shift cycles, start dates, one-day changes, and notes
  • Reminder times, scheduled on this phone
  • Coin balance and shop unlocks
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and ShiftLens Pro

Services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and ShiftLens Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/R252.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

Sharing a week writes an image on this phone and hands it to the share sheet you choose. Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.

# Google Play listing — ShiftLens

Package: `com.ShiftLensMNG.ShiftLens`

## Title

ShiftLens

## Short description

A shift calendar. Set the cycle once, then change only the days that move.

## Full description

People who work shifts do not live on a Monday to Friday calendar. Pick a rotation such as 2 day, 2 night, 2 off, tap the start date, and ShiftLens fills the months.

Change one day when a shift moves. The rest of the cycle stays put. Get a reminder before a shift, and see the week ahead as a colour strip.

Keep a second schedule on the same phone and find the days you both have off. Share the week as an image.

Looks, keep-screen-on, tap sound, and ShiftLens Pro (more than 8 schedules) unlock with coins from the daily reward or 10 coin packs. Remove ads and ShiftLens Pro are one-time unlocks.
